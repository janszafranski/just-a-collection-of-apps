Software you already know how to use.

For every creative and every beginner who wants to make something, but hasn't the time to learn a powerful, complicated suite. Each program works like the next — if it's a website one day and the local newsletter the next, knowing one means you already know the other.

I built these because I was an avid user of the British company Serif. I made all my work in their programs, and since they disappeared I've missed them terribly. I suspect you have too.

So, in a cold room, I rebuilt their successors from the ground up — so I could use them again, this time on my Linux machine.

— Jan

Two small families, one way of working.
