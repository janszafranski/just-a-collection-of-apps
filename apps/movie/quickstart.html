Your first film in six steps

1 — Start a new movie

  1. Open MOVIEeditor. In the Startup wizard pick 1280×720 (HD) and 30 fps, then Start new movie.

2 — Import your media

  1. Click Import media… (Ctrl+I), choose your clips, photos and a piece of music, and click Open.

3 — Build the timeline

  1. Double-click each clip in the order you want it — they line up on the V1 track. Double-click the music to drop it on A1.
  2. Drag clip edges to trim; put the playhead in a clip and press Ctrl+K to split.

4 — Add polish

  1. Right-click the second of two touching clips ▸ Transition ▸ Dissolve.
  2. Timeline ▸ Add title… for an opening caption; drag it onto the overlay track.
  3. Right-click a photo ▸ Ken Burns for a slow zoom.

5 — Preview

  1. Click Build & Play preview to watch it; click ■ to stop and keep editing.

6 — Export

  1. File ▸ Export movie (MP4)… (Ctrl+E), pick 720p, choose a name, and Save. Your film lands in Pictures ▸ just a….
TipSave your project as you go with Ctrl+S (a .movp file) so you can come back and keep editing — that is separate from exporting the movie.

For everything else — speed, effects, colour, picture-in-picture, chroma key, audio pan and more — see the full User Guide.
