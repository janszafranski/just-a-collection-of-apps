1Welcome to PAINTcanvas

PAINTcanvas is a photo and image editor in the tradition of Serif PhotoPlus. You paint and draw, retouch photographs, and apply adjustments and filters — working in layers, just like the program it replaces.

This Quick Start takes you from opening a photo to saving a finished picture. For the full reference, open Help ▸ User Guide.

2Opening or creating an image

File ▸ Open loads a photo (PNG, JPEG and more); File ▸ New makes a blank canvas at a size and background you choose. You can also drag a picture straight in. Saved work goes by default to your Pictures/just a… folder so the launcher can find it again.

3The workspace

The toolbox runs down the left; your image fills the canvas; the Colour picker and the Layers panel sit to the right, with the tool’s options along the top.

4Zooming and the canvas

Zoom with Ctrl+scroll or the zoom control, and pan with the Hand tool. Image menu commands resize the picture, change the canvas, crop, straighten, rotate and flip.

5Painting and drawing

Pick the Brush or Pencil and paint; the Eraser rubs out. Draw filled rectangles and ellipses, pour colour with the Fill, and add words with the Text tool. The tool options bar sets size, hardness and opacity.

6Choosing colours

The Colour picker sets the colour you paint with — drag the sliders or type a hex value. The eyedropper lifts a colour from anywhere in the picture.

7Selections

The rectangular and elliptical selection tools mark off part of the image so an edit only affects that area. With nothing selected, an edit applies to the whole layer.

8Working in layers

Layers stack like sheets of acetate. Add one for new paint, so you never touch the photo beneath. The Layers panel sets each layer’s visibility, opacity and blend mode, and lets you reorder them.

9Adjusting colour

The Adjust menu corrects and transforms colour — brightness and contrast, levels, hue and saturation, colour balance, temperature, and one-click looks like greyscale, sepia and invert.

10Filters

The Filter menu reworks the whole image — blur and sharpen, or creative looks like emboss and find-edges.

11Retouching

The retouch tools fix real photos: Clone paints over a blemish with another part of the picture, Red-eye removes flash red, and Dodge and Burn lighten or darken by hand.

12Saving and exporting

File ▸ Save writes a PNG (which keeps transparency) or a JPEG. Need vector shapes instead? Edit in DRAWmaster hands the picture to the drawing app and back.

13Where to go next

That is the round trip: open a photo → paint, select and layer → adjust and filter → retouch → save. The User Guide covers every tool in depth, and the other apps in the family work the same way.
