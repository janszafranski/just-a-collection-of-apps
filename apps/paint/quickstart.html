1Welcome to PAINTcanvas

PAINTcanvas is a raster image editor: you paint, retouch and adjust pictures made of pixels, then save a PNG or JPEG. It works the way Serif's PhotoPlus did — paint, correct, filter, layer and cut out.

This Quick Start takes you from a blank canvas to a finished, cut-out graphic in a dozen short steps. For the complete reference — every tool, worked projects and a full menu guide — open the User Guide (Help ▸ User Guide).

2Start a picture

Choose File ▸ New… and pick a size — or tick Transparent background for a logo or cut-out. Or File ▸ Open… an existing photo.

3Meet the tools

The vertical tool palette holds every tool — brush, eraser, shapes, fill, gradient, the eyedropper, text, clone, red-eye, dodge and burn, the selection marquees, and the Cutout tool.

4Paint and draw

Set a Colour and Size on the paint toolbar, then draw: the Brush for freehand strokes, the Line, Rectangle and Ellipse for shapes, the Fill bucket for solid areas, and the Gradient for a smooth fade.

5Retouch

Clean a photo up with the Clone stamp (Ctrl-click a source, then paint over the blemish), fix flash eyes with Red-eye, and brush light or dark with Dodge and Burn.

6Adjust colour and tone

The Adjust menu corrects a picture: Brightness / Contrast, Levels, Hue / Saturation, Colour Temperature and one-click Auto Contrast, Greyscale and Sepia.

7Apply a filter

The Filter menu changes texture: Gaussian Blur to soften, Unsharp Mask to sharpen, Median to despeckle, and artistic effects like Find Edges and Emboss.

8Work in layers

The Layers panel stacks sheets of picture. Add a layer, set its opacity and a blend mode, and mix it with the layers below — all non-destructively.

9Select an area

The rectangle and ellipse marquee tools fence off part of the picture, so the next adjustment or filter affects only that area. Ctrl+D deselects when you are done.

10Cut out a background

Open Image ▸ Cutout Studio…, set a tolerance, and click the background with the Cutout tool — or let it remove whatever touches the edges. The background becomes transparent.

11Add a finishing effect

With your subject on transparency, add a soft Effects ▸ Drop Shadow… or Outer Glow… behind it — the touch that lifts a logo or cut-out off the page.

12Save and share

Save with File ▸ Save As…: a PNG keeps transparency and loses no quality; a JPEG is smaller, for photos to share. Your file lands in the shared Pictures/just a… folder, ready for the other just a… apps.

13Where to go next

That is the whole round trip: new or open → paint and retouch → adjust and filter → layer, cut out and finish → save. The User Guide covers every feature in depth, with worked projects and a full reference. And the other just a… apps work alongside this one — a picture saved here is one click away in PAGEdesigner, WEBcreator and DRAWmaster.
