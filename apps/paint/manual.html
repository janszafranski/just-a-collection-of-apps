1. The workspace

PAINTcanvas is a layer-based image editor — paint and draw, retouch photos, apply adjustments and filters, and work in layers, then save a PNG or JPG.

The left rail holds the tools; the top toolbar sets the colour and brush size and the zoom; the menus run File · Edit · Image · Adjust · Filter · Select · Layer · Effects; and the Layers panel is on the right.

2. Starting & saving

File ▸ New picks a size (or a social/A4 preset) and a background — including a transparent background for logos, cut-outs and drop shadows. Open loads a PNG/JPG/BMP/GIF/WEBP. Save writes a PNG or JPG, flattening the layers. Images default to your ~/Pictures/just a… folder, so they turn up in the CREATORsuite hub.

3. Painting & drawing

Set a colour (the Colour button) and a Size, then use:

4. Retouching

The photo-retouch tools, from PhotoPlus:

  • Clone — hold Ctrl and click to set a source point, then paint to copy it elsewhere (great for removing blemishes or duplicating detail).
  • Red-eye — click a red pupil to neutralise it.
  • Dodge (lighten) and Burn (darken) — brush over an area to brighten or shade it.

5. Selections

The two marquee tools (rectangle and ellipse) draw a selection — shown as marching ants. Select ▸ Select All / Deselect are on the menu. While a selection is active, every adjustment, filter and the gradient tool apply only inside it, and Image ▸ Crop to Selection trims to it.

6. Layers

The Layers panel stacks images on top of one another. Use the buttons to add, duplicate, merge down, move up/down and delete a layer; tick the box to show/hide it; drag the Opacity slider; and choose a blend mode (Normal, Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Darken, Lighten, Add, Difference). Double-click a layer to rename it. Painting and adjustments affect the active layer; saving flattens them all.

Blend modes + opacity are the quick way to combine a photo with a texture or a colour wash — drop a layer on top, set it to Multiply or Screen, and dial back the opacity.

7. Adjustments

The Adjust menu changes colour and tone on the active layer (or inside a selection). The slider dialogs preview live:

Brightness / Contrast, Levels, Hue / Saturation, Colour Temperature, Colour Balance, Threshold, Posterize — and one-click Auto Contrast, Equalize, Greyscale, Invert, Sepia.

8. Filters

The Filter menu: Gaussian Blur, Unsharp Mask and Median (Despeckle) with sliders, plus one-click Sharpen, Soften, Detail, Emboss, Find Edges and Contour. Like adjustments, they respect a selection.

9. Image size, crop & straighten

The Image menu: Resize Image (scale the whole picture), Canvas Size (change the working area), Crop to Selection, Straighten (rotate by a small angle and auto-crop), and Rotate 90°/180° and Flip Horizontal/Vertical. These apply to every layer together.

10. Effects

Effects ▸ Drop Shadow and Outer Glow cast a soft, blurred shadow or glow behind whatever you've drawn. They show through transparency, so start from a transparent background (or erase the backdrop first).

11. Working with the other apps

In just a… WEBcreator and PAGEdesigner, right-click a picture (or use the Edit in PAINTcanvas button) to open it here. Save with Ctrl+S and the picture updates in that app automatically. Those apps also have an Adjust Picture menu for quick fixes without leaving them.
