1. The hub

The CREATORsuite launcher is the home for the whole "just a..." family. The left side lists the apps; the right side — Your files — shows everything you have made, so you can jump straight back into a project.

  • Single-click an app's banner to show just that app's files. Click it again to show everything.
  • Double-click a banner to open the app with a blank document.
  • Double-click a file (or select it and press Enter) to open it in its app.

The launcher stays open as a jumping-off point — opening something no longer closes it.

2. Where your files live

Each app saves, by default, into one of two shared folders (created automatically the first time):

FolderHolds
~/Documents/just a...WEBcreator sites (.webc), PAGEdesigner publications (.pagc), DRAWmaster drawings (.svg)
~/Pictures/just a...PAINTcanvas images and DRAWmaster PNG exports (.png, .jpg)

The hub watches these two folders and refreshes the moment you save — your new file simply appears.

3. Recent & importing older work

The Recent strip at the top lists the files you opened last, across every app, newest first. It collapses with the ▾ toggle and filters along with the app selection.

Made things before these folders existed? Click Import older files…. The hub finds your existing WEBcreator / PAGEdesigner / DRAWmaster projects (from the apps' own history and the usual folders) and offers to copy the ones you tick into your "just a..." folders. Your originals are left untouched.

4. QuickShapes

Every design app (WEBcreator, PAGEdesigner and DRAWmaster) now has a gallery of twenty preset shapes beyond the rectangle and ellipse — stars, arrows, speech bubbles, hearts, polygons and more.

In WEBcreator / PAGEdesigner pick one from Insert ▸ QuickShapes, or re-shape any selected box from the shape drop-down on the context toolbar. In DRAWmaster, choose the QuickShape tool and pick a shape, then drag. They stay crisp vectors in PDF and SVG, and publish as SVG on the web.

5. Drop shadow & glow

Give any object a soft drop shadow or outer glow — the Effects… button on the context toolbar (or right-click ▸ Effects). It previews live, prints in PDF, and publishes as a CSS filter on the web.

DRAWmaster has the same Effects button for vector objects, and PAINTcanvas has an Effects menu (Drop Shadow / Outer Glow) that works on a transparent canvas.

6. Lines, borders & arrowheads

Lines and the borders of shapes can be solid, dashed or dotted, and lines can carry arrowheads on either or both ends — all from the context toolbar.

7. Text tools

New in WEBcreator and PAGEdesigner:

  • Drop cap — a big first letter for a paragraph (the ¶ toggle on a text frame's toolbar). The published page wraps the text around a true floated capital.
  • Check Spelling (F7 or Format ▸ Check Spelling) — walks every text frame and lists misspellings with suggestions, and an Add to Dictionary for your own words.
  • Convert to Table — right-click a text frame to turn tab-, comma- or space-separated lines into a table.

8. Transform, calendars & mail merge

Transform… (context toolbar / right-click) lets you type an object's exact position, size and rotation. In PAGEdesigner, Insert ▸ Calendar drops a month calendar as a table, and Insert ▸ Mail Merge reads a CSV and makes one page per record, replacing {{field}} tokens — for letters, labels and cards.

9. DRAWmaster & PAINTcanvas

DRAWmaster gained the QuickShapes gallery and drop-shadow/glow effects, and can finally re-open its own SVGs — the editable drawing is stored inside the file, so nothing is lost.

PAINTcanvas gained the Effects menu (Drop Shadow / Outer Glow). These show through transparency, so New now offers a transparent background for shadows, logos and cut-outs.
