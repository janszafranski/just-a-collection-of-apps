Chapter 1

Getting Started

DRAWmaster is a vector drawing program in the tradition of Serif DrawPlus. A drawing is made of shapes, lines and curves — not pixels — so it stays perfectly sharp at any size and can be re-coloured and reshaped at will. This chapter introduces the workspace.

A fresh canvas

DRAWmaster opens ready to draw on a blank canvas. There is nothing to set up first — pick a tool and begin.

The workspace

The toolbox runs down the left; the drawing fills the centre; the options bar along the top carries the current fill, line width, shape and effect controls.

Saving

File ▸ Save writes an SVG with your editable drawing stored inside it, so you can always re-open and keep working. Drawings save by default to your shared Documents/just a… folder.

Chapter 2

The Canvas and Navigation

Vector work rewards zooming in close and stepping back out. DRAWmaster makes both easy.

Zooming and panning

Zoom with the Zoom + / Zoom − controls or Ctrl+scroll, and pan the canvas when you are zoomed in. Because the drawing is vector, zooming never softens an edge.

Undo and redo

Every action can be undone with Ctrl+Z and redone with Ctrl+Shift+Z, so you can experiment freely.

Chapter 3

Drawing Tools

DRAWmaster builds a picture from a handful of simple tools — geometric shapes, straight lines, and freehand curves.

Rectangles, ellipses and lines

Drag out a rectangle (optionally with rounded corners), an ellipse or a straight line. Hold a modifier to constrain a perfect square, circle or horizontal line.

The pen (polyline)

The pen places points one click at a time, joining them with straight segments — ideal for an angular outline or an arrow. Close the path to make a filled shape.

Freehand paths

The freehand tool draws a smooth path that follows your pointer as you drag, for signatures, doodles and organic curves.

Chapter 4

QuickShapes

QuickShapes are twenty ready-made outlines — drag one onto the canvas and it is yours to resize, recolour and give effects. Every shape below is drawn by the program itself.

The gallery

Using a QuickShape

Pick a shape from the Shape list in the options bar, then drag on the canvas to place it at any size. From there it behaves exactly like a rectangle or ellipse.

Chapter 5

Fills, Outlines and Effects

Colour and effects turn plain outlines into finished artwork.

Fills and outlines

Every shape has a fill (solid colour, or none) and an outline of any colour and width. A line can be dashed. An unfilled shape with a strong outline makes a clean frame.

Drop shadow and glow

Effects add depth: a drop shadow sits a shape above the page, and an outer glow haloes it. Both are adjustable — colour, blur, offset and strength.

Chapter 6

Arranging Objects

A drawing is a stack of shapes. Arranging them — their order, position and grouping — is how a picture comes together.

Selecting, moving and resizing

With the pointer, click a shape to select it and drag to move it; the handles around it resize it. Shift-click to select several at once.

Order and grouping

Order sends a shape to the front or the back so overlaps read correctly, and grouping ties several shapes together so they move and scale as one.

Chapter 7

Colour

DRAWmaster keeps a current fill colour and a current line colour, reused until you change them so a drawing stays consistent.

Setting fill and line colours

Choose the fill and line colours from the colour controls. Select a shape first to recolour it, or set the colours before you draw the next one.

Chapter 8

Saving and Exporting

DRAWmaster’s native format keeps your drawing fully editable, and it exports to the formats the rest of the world uses.

Editable SVG

Save writes an SVG with the editable drawing embedded, so re-opening restores every shape. The same file also displays in any web browser, infinitely scalable.

PNG export and PAINTcanvas

Export a PNG when you need a fixed-size bitmap, or choose Edit in PAINTcanvas to send the drawing to the image editor and paint over it.
