1Welcome to DRAWmaster

DRAWmaster is a vector drawing program: you build a picture out of shapes, lines and paths that stay perfectly sharp at any size, then save it as an SVG or export a PNG. It works in the tradition of Serif's DrawPlus.

This Quick Start takes you from a blank canvas to a finished drawing in a dozen short steps. For the complete reference — every tool, worked projects and a full menu guide — open the User Guide (Help ▸ User Guide).

2Start a drawing

DRAWmaster opens onto a blank canvas. To start fresh at a chosen size, choose File ▸ New… (Ctrl+N) and pick a size — a general canvas, an A4 page, full-HD, or a square.

3The workspace

Your canvas sits in the middle, the tools down the left, and the Style toolbar — fill, line, width, shape, effects and zoom — across the top.

4Meet the tools

The tool palette holds everything you draw with: Select, Freehand, Pen, Line, Rectangle, Ellipse and QuickShape.

5Draw a shape

Choose the Rectangle or Ellipse tool and drag a box on the canvas. The shape appears in the current fill and outline, ready to adjust.

6Try the QuickShapes

Pick a shape from the Shape list — a star, arrow, heart, speech bubble and more — then drag a box and it is drawn to fit. There are eighteen in all.

7Lines, freehand and the pen

The Line tool draws a straight segment; Freehand follows your pointer like a pencil; the Pen builds a shape point by point — double-click to close and fill it, or press Enter to leave it as an open line.

8Colour it in

Click Fill for the colour inside a shape and Line for its outline, then set the Width of the outline (0 for none). With an object selected, the change applies at once.

9Select, move and delete

Switch to the Select tool. Click an object to highlight it, drag to move it, and press Delete to remove it. Edit ▸ Undo (Ctrl+Z) reverses any mistake.

10Add a shadow or glow

With an object selected, click Effects…. Tick drop shadow for depth or outer glow for a halo — or both — and set each one's spread, strength and colour.

11Save and export

Choose File ▸ Save as SVG… (Ctrl+S) to keep an editable master, and File ▸ Export PNG… for a ready-to-use bitmap to drop into a document, website or email.

12Where to go next

That is the whole round trip: new canvas → shapes, lines and paths → colour and effects → save and export. The User Guide covers every feature in depth, with worked projects and a full reference. And the other just a… apps work the same way — once you know one, you know them all.
